## Following a line

Following a line by driving parallel to it at a certain distance is coming problem.

Both following wall or simply avoiding obstacles.

Proportional controller

Turining proportional to the distance from line:
$$a_d=-K_d d$$

Heading parallel to the line
$$a_h=K_h(\theta^*-\theta)$$

Overall steering control law
$$\gamma=-K_d d+K_h(\theta^*-\theta)$$

Heading parallel to line:
$$\theta^*=\tan^{-1}\frac{-a}{b}$$

Distance to line:
$$d=distance(ax+by+c=0,(x^\circ,y^\circ))=\frac{\left|ax^\circ+bx^\circ+c\right|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$$

## Following a path
Error to the tracking point (donkey carrot)
$$e=\sqrt{(x^*-x^\circ)^2+(y^*-y^\circ)^2}-d$$
Regulating e to zero by
$$v^*=K_v e+K_i\int e dt$$
Heading towards the moving goal
$$\theta^*=\tan^-1\frac{y^*-y^\circ}{x^*-x^\circ}$$
Steering proportional to the heading error
$$a=K_h(\theta^*-\theta)$$
