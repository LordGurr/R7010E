## Rotational dynamics

Propeller thrust

$$T_i=b\bar{\omega}$$

Rolling Torque

$$\tau_x=dT^4-dT^2$$
$$\tau_x=db(\bar{\omega}^2_4-\bar{\omega}^2_2)$$

Pitch Torque

$$\tau_y=db(\bar{\omega}^2_1-\bar{\omega}^2_3)$$

Yaw Torque

$$\tau_z=k(\bar{\omega}^2_1+\bar{\omega}^2_2+\bar{\omega}^2_3+\bar{\omega}^2_4)$$

De fulla ekvationerna är ganska stora.

## Cascade control design

Använder tre controllers

- Position controller + Attitude controller
- Yaw controller
- Altitude controller

De matar alla in i control mixer som sen skickar till motorerna på quad rotor

Control mixer och attitude controller kommer inbyggt i din flight controller. Därmed så kan vi ignorera den delen.

If UAV starts rotationg around yaw axis that is sign of world error being used directly without being rotated to body view.
